DESCRIPTION
You've heard the story: Kennedy stared down Khrushchev, and Khrushchev blinked. But that's not how the Cuban Missile Crisis really ended.

In October 1962, the world came closer to nuclear war than ever before. In this video we follow all thirteen days: the missiles in Turkey, the secret Soviet operation to ship nuclear missiles to Cuba, the U-2 spy plane that found them, Kennedy's naval quarantine, Black Saturday, the Soviet submarine officer Vasili Arkhipov, and the secret deal that finally ended the crisis.
