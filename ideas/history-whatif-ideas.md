# Channel Ideas: History & "What If" (Whiteboard Stickman)

Format: long-form 16:9 videos (5–10 min), each with 1–3 vertical Shorts (under 60 s) cut from it.
Style: `whiteboard` template.

## Recurring series

| Series | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **What If…?** | Alternate history: one changed event, then the consequences | Strong curiosity hook, great titles, endless topics |
| **History's Dumbest…** | Absurd wars, inventions, decisions | Comedy fits stickmen; every item can be its own Short |
| **How It Really Happened** | Myth-busting a famous event | Shorts that open with "Everyone thinks… but" do well |
